---
name: execution-readiness
description: Checks whether substantial end-to-end execution or a fresh-agent handoff is sufficiently specified and free of material human-owned blockers. Use before execution begins to surface and resolve gaps early.
---

Trace the intended execution from start to finish and identify material gaps, dependencies, assumptions, and human-owned decisions.

Investigate anything answerable from the repository or available evidence. Use reasonable defaults for non-blocking ambiguity.

For genuine blockers, ask one question at a time. Ask the highest-leverage question first, provide your recommended answer with a brief rationale, then reassess after each response.

Stop when execution can proceed safely with no unresolved material blocker. Summarize readiness, resolved decisions, remaining assumptions, and the next step.
