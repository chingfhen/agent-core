## Targeted maintenance

Markers/headings/labels and the initial cue are defaults. Maintain confirmed paths
and affected routing only; preserve human-customized behavior, outside content,
other owners' blocks, and position. Reread before mutation; refuse duplicate,
incomplete, nested, or ambiguous markers and unsafe paths. Reconcile concurrent
edits safely, then read back and check the owned diff and actual locators.
Report partial routing failures while retaining valid captured material.

Migrating a central workspace block needs identified scope and approval. Preserve
custom text and recorded path semantics; never leave competing destination tables.
